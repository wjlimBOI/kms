Privacy Policy Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the Key Master System ("System", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects the personal information of external users ("you") who access our facilities and use the System to borrow physical keys. This policy is designed to comply with the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) of Singapore and aligns with the guidelines set out by the Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA). It applies solely to data collected through the System.

1. Data Collection

We practise data minimisation – we collect only the information strictly necessary to manage the borrowing and return of physical keys:

  • Full Name – to identify you and contact you regarding your key loan.
  • Email Address – to send you confirmation, reminders, and important notifications.
  • Expected Return Date – to track when the key should be returned.

We do not collect phone numbers, physical addresses, payment card details, or government‑issued IDs.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively for:

  • Logging the issuance and return of physical keys.
  • Sending operational emails (return reminders, fee notifications).
  • Managing lost key reports and replacement fees.
  • Ensuring accountability and security of our key inventory.

We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, or any secondary purpose. We never sell or rent your personal information.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

Access is strictly limited to authorised internal administrators. We do not share your data with external parties, except:

  • If required by law, court order, or governmental regulation.
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of our organisation or users.

We do not transfer personal data outside of Singapore without your explicit consent and only when adequate protections are in place.

4. Data Retention

  • Active loans: Retained for the duration of the loan, plus a reasonable period for audit.
  • Lost key records: Retained for up to 3 years.
  • Upon request, we will securely delete or anonymise your data, subject to legal obligations.

5. Your Rights

Under the PDPA, you have the right to:

  • Access – request a copy of your data.
  • Rectification – correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure – request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention).
  • Withdraw consent – withdraw consent for processing (subject to operational necessity).

To exercise these rights, contact us at wjlim@beautyone.com.sg.

6. Data Security

  • Encrypted transmission (HTTPS).
  • Role‑based access controls.
  • Regular security reviews and monitoring.
  • Data breach response procedures in accordance with PDPA requirements.

7. Children's Privacy

Our System is not intended for individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this policy. We will notify you of material changes via the System or email. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

Terms of Service

By using the System to request and borrow physical keys, you agree to these Terms.

1. User Obligations

  • Provide accurate information (name, email, return date).
  • Return the key by the expected return date.
  • Keep the key secure and in good condition.
  • Report a lost key immediately via the System.
  • Notify us if your contact information changes.

2. Lost Key Protocol

If you lose a key, you must:

  1. Log in to the System using your email address.
  2. Navigate to the "Report Lost Key" section.
  3. Select the lost key from your active loans.
  4. Confirm the report.

Upon confirmation, the key is marked lost and a Key Replacement Fee is applied.

Key Replacement & Administrative Restitution Fee – SGD 50

A non‑negotiable fee of SGD 50 (GST inclusive) applies per lost key. This fee is automatically applied upon reporting and must be paid within 14 days. Failure to pay may result in suspension of borrowing privileges and referral to collections.

Payment: Instructions are sent via email. The fee is non‑refundable except in cases of administrative error.

We do not guarantee uninterrupted System access. We are not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages arising from your use of the System or key loans. Our total liability shall not exceed the replacement fee you have paid.
